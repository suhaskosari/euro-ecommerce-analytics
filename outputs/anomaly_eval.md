## Reading the result

- Recall is measured per day, so partially detected multi-day incidents are visible as misses; 'Incidents caught' is the looser per-incident view.
- Precision is limited by *unlabelled* real structure in the synthetic data (Nov/Dec seasonality, Poisson noise), so some 'false positives' are statistically genuine outliers that were never injected. Treat precision as a lower bound on practical usefulness, not as a pure error rate.
- STL is fitted on the full series (retrospective). A live alerting job needs a causal detector (e.g. an online seasonal baseline), which would score lower than this offline figure.
- With only 5 ground-truth days these metrics have wide uncertainty; they show the detectors' relative behaviour, not a production accuracy estimate.
